# 🛒 ShopVista – Online Store Sales Dashboard | Power BI

## 1. Project Title / Headline

# 🛍️ ShopVista: Online Store Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

An interactive and visually engaging Power BI dashboard designed to analyze online store performance, monitor sales and profitability, and compare business performance across different regions and customer segments.

## 2. Short Description / Purpose

The **ShopVista Online Store Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence solution developed using Microsoft Power BI to provide insights into online retail sales and profitability.

The dashboard helps businesses monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), evaluate monthly sales trends, compare current and previous performance, and analyze regional sales and profit distribution.

It enables business owners, sales managers, and decision-makers to make data-driven decisions, identify business opportunities, and improve overall sales performance.

### A. Business Problem

Online retail businesses generate large amounts of sales data, making it challenging to monitor performance, track profitability, and identify which regions contribute the most to business growth.

Business stakeholders need a clear and interactive way to answer questions such as:

- What is the total sales revenue generated?
- How much profit is the business making?
- How does current performance compare with previous performance?
- Which regions contribute the most to sales and profit?
- How do monthly sales trends change across different countries?
- Which customer segments contribute to business performance?

Without an interactive reporting solution, analyzing these metrics can be time-consuming and difficult.

### B. Goal of the Dashboard

The main objective of the ShopVista dashboard is to provide a centralized and interactive reporting solution that:

- Tracks overall sales and profit performance.
- Compares current results with previous performance.
- Analyzes sales trends across different countries.
- Provides insights into regional sales and profitability.
- Helps monitor business performance across customer segments.
- Supports better strategic and data-driven business decisions.

### C. Walkthrough of Key Visuals

**1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**

The dashboard highlights important business metrics:

- **Total Profit:** 929.98K
- **Previous Profit:** 669.87K
- **Profit Comparison:** 260.12K
- **Total Sales:** 7.43M
- **Previous Sales:** 6.12M
- **Sales Comparison:** 1.31M

These KPIs provide a quick overview of the business's financial performance.

**2. Month Filter**

An interactive month slicer allows users to select a specific month and analyze sales and profit performance for the selected period.

**3. Regional Sales & Profit Analysis**

A horizontal bar chart presents regional performance using sales and profit metrics.

It helps identify high-performing and low-performing regions and understand their contribution to overall business performance.

**4. Sales Per Month / Country Analysis**

A line chart visualizes sales performance across different countries:

- United States of America – 1.62M
- Canada – 1.59M
- Germany – 1.39M
- France – 1.33M
- Mexico – 1.03M

This visualization makes it easier to compare sales performance across countries and identify important markets.

**5. Customer Segment Analysis**

The dashboard includes interactive business segment filters:

- Channel Partners
- Enterprise
- Government
- Midmarket
- Small Business

These filters allow users to explore business performance across different customer segments.

**6. Interactive Dashboard Design**

The dashboard features a modern dark-blue theme, interactive filters, KPI cards, and graphical visualizations to provide a user-friendly analytical experience.

### D. Business Impact & Insights

The ShopVista dashboard supports business decision-making in several ways:

- **Sales Performance Monitoring:** Helps businesses track revenue and monitor overall sales growth.
- **Profitability Analysis:** Provides insights into profit generation and financial performance.
- **Regional Performance:** Helps identify countries and regions contributing significantly to sales.
- **Customer Segmentation:** Enables businesses to analyze performance across different customer segments.
- **Performance Comparison:** Makes it easier to compare current and previous sales and profit figures.
- **Strategic Decision-Making:** Supports better planning, sales optimization, and business growth strategies.
